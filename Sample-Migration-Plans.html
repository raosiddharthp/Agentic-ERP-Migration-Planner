Sample Migration Plans

These examples illustrate typical outputs generated by the Agentic ERP Migration Planner for common migration scenarios. All plans are hypothetical but based on real-world patterns and benchmarks.

Example 1: SAP ECC → S/4HANA (Global Manufacturing Company)

Input Summary

  • Current system: SAP ECC 6.0 with 8,000+ custom Z-objects
  • Modules: FI, CO, MM, SD, PP, QM, WM
  • Data volume: 2.5 TB, 15 years history
  • Integrations: 45 third-party systems
  • Users: 5,000 across 12 countries

Recommended Approach: Phased Brownfield

Convert existing ECC system to S/4HANA while selectively innovating with new functionality.

Phased Plan

Phase Scope Duration Go-Live
Phase 1 Finance & Controlling (global template) 9 months Q3 2026
Phase 2 Procurement & Manufacturing (Europe & NA) 12 months Q3 2027
Phase 3 Sales & Distribution + remaining regions 10 months Q2 2028

Key Risks Identified

  • High: Custom code volume — recommend selective clean-core approach
  • Medium: Multi-country rollout — language and legal requirements
  • Medium: Data archiving strategy for historical data

Estimated Cost

$28–35 million over 30 months (including licensing, consulting, internal resources)

Example 2: Oracle EBS → Oracle Cloud ERP (Mid-Size Retail Chain)

Input Summary

  • Current system: Oracle EBS 12.1
  • Modules: Financials, Supply Chain, HR
  • Data volume: 800 GB
  • Customizations: Moderate (1,200 custom objects)
  • Users: 800 across 3 countries

Recommended Approach: Hybrid Lift-and-Shift with Selective Reimplementation

Phased Plan

Phase Scope Duration
Phase 1 Financials (lift-and-shift) 8 months
Phase 2 Supply Chain reimplementation 10 months
Phase 3 HR and remaining functions 6 months

Key Risks Identified

  • High: Integration with POS systems
  • Medium: Change management for supply chain processes

Estimated Cost

$12–16 million over 24 months

Example 3: Legacy Custom ERP → Dynamics 365 (Financial Services Firm)

Input Summary

  • Current system: 20-year-old custom COBOL mainframe
  • Heavy regulatory reporting requirements
  • Data volume: 1.2 TB
  • Users: 1,200

Recommended Approach: Parallel Run with Gradual Cutover

Key Considerations

  • Run new D365 system in parallel for 6 months
  • Regulatory reporting validated on both systems
  • Gradual user migration by department

Estimated Timeline

36 months total (including 6-month parallel run)
